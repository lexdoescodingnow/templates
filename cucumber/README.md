## Editing

Literal `[url]`, `[name]` and `[text]` fields appear at the top of every snippet. The GIF URLs and lorem ipsum follow, before the decorative markup and stylesheet link. The preview displays Tao & Zhiyuan and sample titles. **Copy template** keeps the literal fields; **Copy for Tao & Zhiyuan** substitutes the displayed names and title. Edits update the preview and copied code together.

The preview editor accepts `[b]`, `[i]` and `[u]` and converts them into the matching HTML tags for the `[dohtml]` snippet. Bold and underline use the forward member-colour gradient; italics reverse it. Hand-edited HTML can use `<b>` / `<strong>`, `<i>` / `<em>` and `<u>` directly.

The templates inherit `--mgrgb1`, `--mgrgb2` and `--mgrgb3` from the forum, including their headings, frames, borders and device accents. Preview palettes stay in the preview. Surfaces remain neutral, with small muted cucumber motifs. Blue Hour's `html[color-mode="light"]` and `html[color-mode="dark"]` take precedence over the system colour preference. Common alternate theme classes and attributes are supported too.

Comms messages are ordinary `<p>` paragraphs, including successive opening `<p>` tags without closing tags. `data-direction` accepts `received`, `sent` and `alternating`. The device controls are decorative. Buds contain short sample replies for roughly 100 words or fewer, and expand if more text is added.

Clear a GIF field to remove that portrait. Both slots remain available in the editor, including designs that start without pictures. For hand edits, remove the corresponding `cu-shot` span. Empty media areas collapse. Adjust `--cu-crop` to move the image within its frame.

The forum snippets use one direct stylesheet link, no JavaScript and no CSS imports. The browser preview embeds its stylesheet and editor. GIFs and posted-template CSS load online. There are no comments, hidden tips or editing notes inside the template markup or stylesheet.

## Rebuilding

Run `node cucumber/build.cjs`, `python tools/build_forum_posts.py`, `node cucumber/verify.cjs` and `python cucumber/package.py`. The forum builder retains Cucumber's requested placeholders and lorem ipsum in copy boxes, while rendering named examples.
