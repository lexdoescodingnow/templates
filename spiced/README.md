## Copy and edit

Open any named `.txt` file on GitHub, choose **Raw**, and copy the complete `[dohtml]` block into your post. It includes the hosted stylesheet link. The URL, name, title, GIFs, and writing appear before the decorative markup and stylesheet. There are no comments, hidden tips, or editing notes in the posting snippets or CSS.

Replace `[url]`, `[name]`, and `[text]` with the link, character names, and title or status. Both supplied Tumblr GIF URLs are filled in, along with lorem ipsum. Replace a GIF URL, remove either image line, or remove the whole GIF container. The inline `object-position` controls the crop.

Download the preview HTML and open it in your browser. Browse all fifteen designs with the type tabs, named choices, or Previous/Next. The editor includes names, links, titles, GIF URLs and crops, writing, member palette tests, and Light/Dark/System modes. Edits stay with each design during the current page session. Copy and Download output a complete posting snippet. Original snippets are also available in a disclosure. CSS is embedded for the preview; portraits and Google Fonts load online.

Comms use plain `<p>` paragraphs, one per message. Successive opening `<p>` tags work without closing tags. Change `data-direction="received"` to `sent` for outgoing messages, and edit the time and receipt text. Handset details are decorative. Bud samples contain fewer than 100 words; the editor counts words without limiting your reply.

The editor accepts `[b]`, `[i]`, and `[u]` and converts them to HTML. Inside `[dohtml]`, use `<b>`, `<i>`, and `<u>`; `<strong>` and `<em>` are supported too. Bold and underline follow group 1 → 2 → 3. Italics reverse the gradient to group 3 → 2 → 1.

## Styling

All snippets load `spiced-collection-v1.css` through jsDelivr, with styles scoped to `.spc`. No forum JavaScript or IDs are required. Blue Hour's inherited `--mgrgb1`, `--mgrgb2`, and `--mgrgb3` comma-separated RGB variables drive the colour scheme. Preview colour choices stay out of copied code.

Explicit `html[color-mode="light"]` and `html[color-mode="dark"]` override the operating system preference. System mode is used only when that attribute is absent. Bodoni Moda and Manrope load from Google Fonts with local fallbacks. Spice engravings and corner ornaments are original SVG masks embedded in the stylesheet.

## Designs

- **Saffron Nocturne (thread):** An intimate double portrait, ornamental corners, and fine nocturne borders.
- **Cassia Cabinet (thread):** A slender cabinet of portraits beside the writing, with engraved cinnamon details.
- **Anise Reverie (thread):** Octagonal portraits and an anise rosette above an italic title.
- **Cardamom Veil (thread):** A broad portrait with an inset companion, asymmetric borders, and cardamom engraving.
- **Clove Sonata (thread):** Opposing portrait curves, a framed title, and a delicate clove finish.
- **Pepperline (comms):** A classic handset with paired contact coins and peppercorn detailing.
- **Cassia Calling (comms):** A squared handset with stacked contact portraits and a serif contact name.
- **Saffron Signal (comms):** A panoramic contact banner with saffron tendrils and soft message bubbles.
- **Anise Afterhours (comms):** An ornamental night phone with octagonal portraits and anise detailing.
- **Cardamom Connection (comms):** A compact contact panel with layered portraits and a framed message area.
- **Emberkiss (bud):** A miniature ornamental frame with portraits tucked beside the title.
- **Clovelet (bud):** A tiny portrait column and a clove engraving beside a short reply.
- **Saffron Wisp (bud):** A light little reply with a cinematic portrait ribbon underneath.
- **Anise Spark (bud):** Two small octagonal portraits in the margin of a miniature note.
- **Pepperpin (bud):** A centred title, two portrait coins, and a scatter of peppercorns.
